Can it beat copying Malmö?

A linear regression learned two weights and an offset from 182 matched readings in August–October 2021. The test used the next 73 readings, from 1 November through 7 December. Only Malmö and Skillinge temperatures entered the model.

MethodMAE (°C)RMSE (°C)
Copy Malmö0.4740.670
Two-station regression0.4010.586

Mean absolute error (MAE) is the average size of a miss. The model cut it by 15.5%, about 0.07 °C per reading. Root mean squared error (RMSE) gives large misses more weight.

Where it missed

On 3 November at 06:00 UTC, Malmö measured 4.1 °C and Skillinge 6.7 °C. The model leaned towards Malmö and estimated 4.25 °C. Lund measured 6.1 °C, the largest miss of the test period.

The experiment covers about five weeks at 06:00 and 18:00 UTC. Testing more years and hours would show how the model handles summer heat and daytime outages.
