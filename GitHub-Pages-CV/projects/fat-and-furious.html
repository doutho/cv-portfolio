Fat and Furious

LTH, Faculty of Engineering, Lund University

Overview

I co-developed Fat and Furious, a VR boss-battle game for Meta Quest 3, as part of a four-person team in a virtual reality course at LTH, Lund University. The project used Unity and Blender, incorporating existing assets to create a playable boss-battle experience.
